Emira

Aubrey

A quiet journey through a strange house.

Coming soon to iOS and Steam.

Aubrey is a finite, story-driven incremental clicker with point-and-click escape-room elements. Build Momentum, tend to meaningful objects, search a strange neglected house, and craft solutions to help a small girl move forward.

Aubrey is a finite incremental clicker woven together with point-and-click exploration and escape-room puzzles.

You don’t control Aubrey directly. Instead, you guide her attention toward objects and places throughout a strange, neglected house. Time your breaths to generate Momentum, then use it to tend meaningful objects, investigate the environment, uncover hidden items, craft solutions, and explore deeper into the house.

When Aubrey reaches her limit, she must return to her bed and sleep. Her temporary Momentum and producer levels reset, while discoveries, solved puzzles, opened rooms, and permanent progress remain. Each new beginning becomes a little easier, allowing her to reach farther into the house.

This is not an endless clicker. Aubrey is a complete journey with secrets to uncover and a definite conclusion—a quiet story about childhood, comforting rituals, painful memories, and the Momentum required to take another step.

Content warning: Aubrey contains mature themes, emotionally distressing subject matter, and unsettling imagery. Player discretion is advised.

If something isn’t working, or you have a question, email emira.support@gmail.com.

Moving to another Apple device

Choose “Use iCloud” during onboarding on the new device, or turn on “Sync with iCloud” in Settings. Emira looks for the private library in the same Apple Account’s iCloud Drive, including attached photos.

Backups

You can export a backup from Settings and restore it later. iCloud is optional; Emira stays local by default.

Notifications and music

Both are optional. You can change notification timing, ambient-music playback, and background playback in Settings at any time.

For faster help, include the Emira version, your iPhone or iPad model, and a short description of what happened.

What Emira stores

Emira stores the information you choose to enter: your birth date and life settings; goals; milestones; journal entries; Time Capsules; personalization settings; and any photos you choose to attach. By default, this information stays on your device.

Optional iCloud Drive sync

If you turn on iCloud in Emira, the app stores a private, versioned copy of your library in iCloud Drive under the Apple Account signed in on your device. This can include your entries, settings, and attached photos so your own Apple devices can stay in step. iCloud is optional and can be turned off in Settings. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Photos and camera

Emira accesses your photo library or camera only when you choose to attach a photo to a memory. It does not access your photos or camera in the background.

Notifications and ambient music

If you allow notifications, Emira schedules gentle reminders and Time Capsule opening reminders locally on your device. It does not use a notification server, collect a device token, or send notification data to Fox & Moss. Ambient tracks are included with the app; turning music or optional background playback on does not create an account or send listening information to Fox & Moss.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Emira does not require an account. It does not show ads, sell personal information, use third-party advertising SDKs, or track you across apps or websites. Fox & Moss does not receive the personal content you create in Emira.

Support email and your choices

If you email emira.support@gmail.com, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private journal content unless necessary. You can edit or remove entries and photos, export or restore a backup, turn iCloud off, and reset data stored on your device from Settings.

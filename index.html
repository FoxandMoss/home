Emira

A place to keep what matters close.

Emira is a calm, highly customizable life-reflection app: a place to see the life you’ve lived, keep a few things you’d like to do close by, and remember the moments that mattered.

Reflection, without pressure

Life Calendar, Goals, Milestones, Journal entries, Time Capsules, and quiet activities. No streaks, overdue goals, or productivity scores.

Yours, once

A single purchase. No subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads.

Made to be personal

Customize themes, icons, backgrounds, music, and more. Your library stays on your device unless you choose iCloud.

Emira Support

We’re here.

If something isn’t working, or you have a question about Emira, email emira.support@gmail.com.

Moving to another Apple device

Choose “Use iCloud” during onboarding on the new device, or turn on “Sync with iCloud” in Settings. Emira looks for the private library in the same Apple Account’s iCloud Drive, including attached photos.

Keep a copy

You can export a backup from Settings and restore it later. iCloud is optional; Emira stays local by default.

Notifications and music

Both are optional. You can change notification timing, ambient-music playback, and background playback in Settings at any time.

For faster help, include the Emira version, your iPhone or iPad model, and a short description of what happened.

Emira Privacy Policy

Your reflections are yours.

Last updated September 6, 2026

Emira is designed to work without an account and without sending your personal library to Fox & Moss. This policy explains what stays on your device, what happens if you choose iCloud, and how optional iOS features work.

What Emira stores

Emira stores the information you choose to enter: your birth date and life settings; goals; milestones; journal entries; Time Capsules; personalization settings; and any photos you choose to attach. By default, this information stays on your device.

Optional iCloud Drive sync

If you turn on iCloud in Emira, the app stores a private, versioned copy of your library in iCloud Drive under the Apple Account signed in on your device. This can include your entries, settings, and attached photos so your own Apple devices can stay in step. iCloud is optional and can be turned off in Settings. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Photos and camera

Emira accesses your photo library or camera only when you choose to attach a photo to a memory. It does not access your photos or camera in the background.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Emira schedules gentle reminders and Time Capsule opening reminders locally on your device. Emira does not use a notification server, collect a device token, or send notification data to Fox & Moss.

Ambient music

Ambient tracks are included with the app. Turning music or optional background playback on does not create an account or send listening information to Fox & Moss.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Emira does not require an account. It does not show ads, sell personal information, use third-party advertising SDKs, or track you across apps or websites. Fox & Moss does not receive the personal content you create in Emira.

Support email

If you email emira.support@gmail.com, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private journal content unless it is necessary to explain a problem.

Your choices

You can edit or remove individual entries and photos in Emira. You can export a backup, restore one, turn iCloud off, and reset all data stored on your device from Settings.

Questions or changes

If you have a privacy question, contact emira.support@gmail.com. If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
